About

I'm a computational scientist working where chemistry meets data. My background spans biotechnology, bioinformatics and organic chemistry, and my work today focuses on cheminformatics, QSAR modelling, generative molecular design and molecular dynamics. I care about models that are not only accurate but explainable, so chemists can trust them and act on them.
